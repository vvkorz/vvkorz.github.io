# Leadership Workshops

Leadership workshop of a large corporate organisation is in full swing. More than 30 participants are actively engaged in discussions. 

Topic at hand: There are a lot of projects and activities and an obvious lack of focus. "Shall we introduce 100 $ to everyone who closes a project?" - someone proses. Excitement raises in the room, there is a suggestion to raise the bar and introduce a bounty program to hunt for projects and kill them. Another person suggests a more methodological approach and proposes to define killer criteria at the beginning of every project. Discussion erupts ... are killer criteria those that define how project should be killed or those that define killer features that make the project successful? Tension ... You open X: SpaceX catched 52.1 meter tall and 9 meter wide Starship successfully...

"We are just really bad at stopping projects!" - someone exclaims. After 30 minutes of discussion comes an action item: *Every project needs to have a definition of done!* Everyone nods... Who is the owner? one proactive person raises a hand and suggest to form a working group that in one month will deliver definition of done for projects. Everyone nods... Let's call it DoD for short. Everyone nods... Top hackernews post is about openai solving another math problem..."

"WE need to stuff project dedicatedly..."
"Top management discusses leadership models. "
